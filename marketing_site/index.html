Why EcoBite

A kitchen app for people who want easier meal decisions.

EcoBite is ideal for students, busy households, beginners in cooking, and anyone who opens the fridge but still does not know what to make. Instead of starting from a blank mind, the app starts from your actual food items.

App Features

Everything is built around using food before it goes to waste.

01

Kitchen Inventory

Save your food items, quantities, categories, and expiry dates in one organized place.

02

Expiry Awareness

Clear color indicators show fresh, expiring, and expired items so important food does not get forgotten.

03

Recipe Suggestions

EcoBite recommends recipes based on what is already available, especially ingredients that should be used soon.

04

Predictive Grocery Plan

The app suggests missing items for recipes and staples, helping users shop with a clearer purpose.

05

AI Food Assistant

Ask food-related questions about storage, cooking ideas, leftovers, waste reduction, and simple recipes.

06

Personal Preferences

Diet, cooking style, household size, meal preferences, reminders, and dark mode make the app feel personal.

For everyday cooking

EcoBite helps when you have ingredients but no meal idea.

Many users waste food not because they do not care, but because they forget what they own or do not know how to combine it. EcoBite connects inventory, recipes, and grocery planning so users can make decisions faster.

Example flow

Rice + chicken + spinach EcoBite suggests a quick rice bowl, reminds you spinach expires soon, and adds only missing ingredients to the grocery plan.
