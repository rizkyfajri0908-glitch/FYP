Why EcoBite

A kitchen app for people who want easier meal decisions.

EcoBite is ideal for students, busy households, beginners in cooking, and anyone who opens the fridge but still does not know what to make. Instead of starting from a blank mind, the app starts from your actual food items.

FAQ

Built for practical daily use

Who is EcoBite useful for?

Students, families, beginner cooks and anyone who wants help deciding what to cook from food they already own.

Does EcoBite only track expiry dates?

No. It connects expiry tracking with recipe suggestions, grocery planning, AI assistance, barcode scanning and personal preferences.

Why is this related to food waste reduction?

EcoBite encourages users to cook older ingredients first, avoid duplicate purchases and plan meals around food that might otherwise be forgotten.
