Application Features

Designed around everyday kitchen decisions.

01

Kitchen Inventory

Add, edit, scan, search, and filter food items while expiry status is shown clearly with color-coded indicators.

02

Recipe Suggestions

Recipes are ranked using available ingredients, missing items, dietary preferences, and expiring food.

03

Predictive Grocery Plan

EcoBite suggests grocery items based on recipe gaps, missing staples, expired replacements, and user profile settings.

04

AI Food Assistant

A focused assistant answers food storage, cooking, recipe, grocery, and food waste reduction questions.

05

Expiry Reminders

Notification settings help users decide when and how often they want reminders before ingredients expire.

06

User Preferences

Dietary needs, cooking style, household size, meal types, dark mode, and food habits personalize the experience.

Sustainability Alignment

Supporting SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.

EcoBite aligns especially with SDG Target 12.3 by helping users reduce consumer-level food waste through expiry tracking, recipe planning, reminders, and smarter grocery decisions.

Target 12.3

Reduce food waste at retail and consumer levels while encouraging efficient use of household food resources.

How It Works

A simple loop for reducing waste.

1

Track

Users add food items manually or through barcode scanning.

2

Prioritize

EcoBite highlights expiring and expired ingredients.

3

Cook

Recipe suggestions encourage users to cook with available ingredients.

4

Plan

The grocery plan helps users buy only what they need next.
